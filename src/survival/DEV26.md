## Autonomous settlers — experimental, not human-level intelligence

- New-world selector: **0–96 settlers**, default 32. Existing dangerous-monument guards are separate. Default new-world size is **1170×1170 m**; 650/910/4000/8000 m remain available. Old saves retain terrain and buildings; legacy saves receive the default settler limit and a one-time society initialization on entry. Simulation advances only while playing; no menu/offline earnings.
- Occupied, non-raided houses are selected by size. Initial capacity is **1 + floor count** (2–6 inhabitants); the global limit bounds the number actually created. House ownership and possessions stay in the save. Three alternating teams are hostile to other teams; households of the same team are allied.
- **Only initialization** grants the explicitly authored depot, tools, clothes, guns, ammunition, food and water. Starting houses/furniture retain the earlier authored-world allowance. Depot inventories are real `tc-ai26-*` containers, visible as external supply crates. Empty depots are never rolled again. This is a deliberate accessible supply cache, not an invulnerable abstract bank.
- Settlers walk to shared finite resource nodes and invoke the same harvesting function with their own inventory, actual tools, shared `depleted`/`nodeDamage`, and ×1 yields. They do not increment the player's inventory or earn arbitrary `botStores` income. They carry materials back before spending them.
- Seek accessible unowned RT/road crates and corpse bags, use the same one-time container rolls, transfer actual quantities, and mark emptied random crates depleted. Safe zones are excluded. Loot access includes a line-of-sight check. Upper-storey/narrow indoor routes are not guaranteed: bounded navigation can abandon inaccessible targets.
- Home processing has an 8-second household clock, requires a member at the depot, consumes wood and up to two units of each available ore, and outputs the corresponding metal/sulfur plus charcoal. This is a bounded simplified NPC furnace process, not a duplicate free crafting-income timer. Ammunition/charges and basic weapon components use the actual shared recipe costs and output quantities. Equipped gun ownership, ammunition, healing and food are finite.
- Builders reserve a nearby dry, low-slope plot outside roads/RT/other homes. They sequentially pay for and instantiate an actual one-cell wooden dwelling: foundation, three walls, doorway/door, roof, storage, cupboard, furnace, workbench. Wood-tier structural cost includes the base plan cost plus the ordinary wood upgrade payment. Maximum two added modules per household, with the existing global building/save budget protected. Extra capacity is recorded; this version does not spawn unlimited new residents as houses grow. Cupboard upkeep is transferred from the real depot.
- Combat requires range and actual world line of sight, plus player-facing/daylight/provocation rules. Roles hold an ambush, flank or attack; poorly supplied/wounded actors retreat. Allies share a short-lived contact location, not omniscient wall-penetrating sight. Settlers can wait outside unsafe RT approaches. Safe-zone players are not attacked. NPC-vs-NPC fire uses LOS-gated probabilistic damage/tracers; shots at the player use the existing projectile callback. These are **not identical combat implementations**.
- Shots consume one real round, healing consumes carried bandages, food consumes carried food. Clothing in an actor's inventory supplies the existing weighted protection calculation. The highest-grade item per clothing slot is used; this is a simplified NPC equipment policy, not the player's full inventory UI.
- Hostile settlers can target player walls/doors outside safe areas as well as rival households (peaceful mode requires player provocation). A raid requires a carried crafted charge, movement to the target, and a four-second fuse. The charge is removed once. Damage is applied through the existing structural-damage/destruction/collapse/content-drop path. Raid explosions are simplified targeted structural damage, not full player explosive-area simulation. Breached/undefended hostile supply caches can be robbed. Raiders do not get manufactured loot as a reward.
- Death transfers the **actual carried inventory** into a persistent shared ground bag; the old fabricated `scrap/fiber/ammo` kill reward is bypassed. Settlers respawn after 180 playing seconds at their depot **without new gear**; resupply comes from surviving supplies. Guards do not gain endless ammunition or respawned loadouts.
- Society actors, stocks, positions, health, food, household plans, raid clocks and the last 32 event messages persist. Save decoding validates bounded counts/coordinates/item IDs/weapon IDs. The HUD's “Жизнь острова” reports residents and recent events without exposing enemy positions.

### Navigation and presentation boundaries

4 m local A*, at most 180 expansions per replan and two replans per rendered frame, collision-checked intermediate movement samples, partial routes and abandoned unreachable goals. Decisions are staggered: nearby agents 4 Hz, distant agents 1 Hz; distant agents still consume/gather real resources. There is no global baked navmesh, learned planning, conversation, diplomacy, boats or expert interior assault. Complex multi-storey RT can be unreachable. No path can teleport a living actor to its loot; respawn is the sole explicit relocation.

Existing licensed Quaternius Swat/Adventurer skinned humans are used, not newly commissioned realistic humans. Procedural bone gait follows movement speed, running uses faster cadence, and armed poses retain a two-arm hold. Armor loss on respawn changes Swat to the lighter Adventurer model. This is not mocap or a complete seven-slot third-person wardrobe; first-person player's clothing rig and world meshes are otherwise retained. Do not call this “complete human intelligence” or validated Rust-like NPC quality.

## Weapons and modules

- Two additional guns: **Карабин К-24** (`carbine26`, 24 rifle rounds) and **ПДВ-40** (`pdw26`, 40 pistol rounds). Registered recipes with mandatory components, military/elite loot, ammunition profiles, signed recoil, pose/reload profiles and existing licensed audio groups.
- Ready-made local **Quaternius CC0** AR_1 / SMG_1 meshes from Sci-Fi Modular Gun Pack, pinned source commit and SHA-256 in `web/assets/models26/manifest.json`. These are stylized meshes, **not realistic military scans**. The adapter normalizes canonical axes/scale, authors UV projections, applies local existing color/normal/roughness maps, and separates lower magazine triangles for existing reload ownership. It keeps sight mounts, muzzle/support-hand sockets and layer setup. No downloading models at runtime. Existing guns keep their authored textured geometry, not an alleged full replacement of every gun with a photorealistic asset.
- Four additional modules, **12 total**: 8× optic, compact suppressor (damage ×.96, suppressed sample/main flash), compensator (recoil ×.84), drum magazine (+75%, original extended-mag compatibility). Original eight modules and four saved slots per weapon type remain. Real item transfer/refund/magazine-capacity code is retained; new module offers and random module rolls use the extended catalog.

## Build/validation policy

Source/asset inspection, `node --check`, `git diff --check`, esbuild compilation, and **build-only** Android APK / Windows portable EXE CI. **No unit/browser/native/emulator/smoke/game/visual/audio/FPS tests, no device launch.** Compilation establishes neither AI survival/economy/pathfinding quality nor correct on-device appearance, grips, collisions or FPS. Experimental autonomous behavior has not been run in a game session. Existing save key/application ID/development signature and world ownership are retained; export saves before a new world.
